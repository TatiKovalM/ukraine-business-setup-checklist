# Ukraine business setup checklist for foreign founders

A working checklist I keep for foreign founders and companies setting up in Ukraine: which vehicle to use, which documents to prepare abroad, and which statutes to read. Statute references were checked against the texts on the Verkhovna Rada portal in late 2026. Corrections and additions are welcome via issues or pull requests.

## 1. Pick the vehicle

| Vehicle | Legal entity | Registrar's deadline | Foreign staff need a work permit | Statute |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LLC (TOV) | Yes | 24 hours, weekends and holidays excluded | Yes, save for the Art. 42(6) exemptions | Law No. 2275-VIII; Law No. 755-IV, Art. 26(1)(1) |
| Representative office of a foreign company | No | 5 working days | No (Law No. 5067-VI, Art. 42(6)(8)) | Civil Code, Art. 95; Law No. 755-IV, Arts. 22, 26(1)(9) |
| Branch of a foreign company | No | 5 working days | No (Art. 42(6)(8)) | Same as above |
| Sole proprietor (FOP) | No (individual) | 24 hours | n/a | Law No. 755-IV, Art. 6(7) |

Notes:

- A single foreign participant may own 100% of an LLC; there is no minimum charter capital (Law No. 2275-VIII, Arts. 4, 12).
- A foreigner can register as a FOP only while lawfully in Ukraine on a document that is the basis for their stay. The remote e-Residency route has been closed to new applicants since September 2025.
- Guide: [how a non-resident registers a company in Ukraine](https://ua4all.org/register-company-ukraine-non-resident.html)
- Guide: [representative office of a foreign company in Ukraine](https://ua4all.org/representative-office-ukraine-foreign-company.html)

## 2. Documents to prepare at home

- [ ] Founders' decision (LLC) or parent company's decision naming the office address and head (representative office or branch)
- [ ] Charter, or a declaration in the application that the model charter is used (LLC)
- [ ] Regulations of the office approved by the parent (representative office or branch)
- [ ] Extract from the home commercial register, **issued no earlier than one month before filing** (Law No. 755-IV, Art. 15(1)(13-1))
- [ ] Ownership-structure schematic in the Ministry of Finance form (Order No. 163 of 19 March 2021)
- [ ] Copy of the ultimate beneficial owner's identity document
- [ ] Apostille or consular legalisation on every foreign document (Art. 15(1)(13))
- [ ] Ukrainian translation with certified accuracy or certified translator's signature (Art. 15(1)(14))
- [ ] Power of attorney for the person filing, notarised or issued under foreign law (Art. 14(2))

## 3. After registration

- [ ] Ukrainian tax number for each foreign individual involved ([how a foreigner gets one](https://ua4all.org/tax-number-ukraine-foreigner.html))
- [ ] Bank account: expect the bank to run its own checks on the ownership chain ([bank account for a foreign-owned company](https://ua4all.org/open-bank-account-ukraine-foreign-company.html))
- [ ] Work permit for a foreign director of an LLC, unless an exemption applies ([work permit for a foreign employee](https://ua4all.org/hire-foreign-employee-ukraine-work-permit.html))
- [ ] Accounting and first reporting deadlines ([bookkeeping for a foreign-owned company](https://ua4all.org/bookkeeping-accounting-ukraine-foreign-owned-company.html))
- [ ] If you run an IT company: check whether Diia.City fits ([Law No. 1667-IX](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/1667-20))
